## Common questions

### How do I delete my account?

Tap your photo or initial at the top left of any tab to open Account, scroll to the bottom, and tap **Delete account**. You'll be asked to type the word "delete" to confirm. Your profile, photos, pets, reports, walks, alert settings, and notification tokens are removed immediately; there's no 30-day waiting period. If you signed up with Sign in with Apple, PawWatch also asks Apple to revoke its access. If you choose **Delete without Apple**, you can remove PawWatch yourself in iOS Settings (your name, Sign-In & Security, Sign in with Apple).

If you no longer have access to the app, email [privacy@paw-watch.app](mailto:privacy@paw-watch.app) from the address you signed up with and we'll delete your account within 30 days.

### How do I report a hazard?

Open PawWatch, tap the **+ Report** button at the bottom of the map, pick a category, set the severity, move the map so the pin sits on the spot, and add a short description if you like. Most reports take under 30 seconds. You can also tap **Flag hazard** during a walk.

### Can I delete a report I made?

Yes. Open your report and tap **Delete report** at the bottom, then confirm. It disappears from the map for everyone, along with its ratings and confirmations.

### Why aren't I getting push notifications?

Three things to check:

- You granted notification permission to PawWatch (iOS Settings → PawWatch → Notifications).
- Hazard alerts are turned on and your radius is set (Alerts tab, or Account → Alert settings), and you've set your home (Account → Change neighborhood).
- You have a working internet connection. Try opening the map to confirm.

Alerts are for new hazards reported by other walkers; you won't get one for your own reports. An alert shows the hazard type and how far it is from your home, and, once the report has passed review, the start of its description; until then it says "Tap to view." instead.

Still stuck? Email [support@paw-watch.app](mailto:support@paw-watch.app) and we'll help you debug.

### Why is my report missing from the map?

Reports expire based on severity: low (24h), medium (72h), high (7d). After that, the row is deleted from our database, not just hidden. New reports are checked automatically before other walkers can see them, which usually takes a few seconds; until then only you can see yours. If you just submitted it and don't see it, try pulling down to refresh the list, or switch tabs and come back to the map.

### Can I use PawWatch without sharing my location?

Yes. Without location, the map centers on your home and you place report pins by moving the map. Alerts use your saved home, so they work without location too. Walk tracking needs location. To turn off location later, go to iOS Settings → PawWatch → Location.

### Why was my report downgraded or hidden?

If multiple walkers report the same hazard as no longer present, or if multiple walkers disagree with the report, PawWatch will auto-deactivate it. We surface the dispute count on the report's detail page so you can see the breakdown.

A report can also be hidden by our automated check or by a moderator. Your own hidden reports show a note in My Activity explaining their status.

### How do I flag a report or block a user?

Open any hazard report and tap **Report this content** at the bottom. You can choose a category (harassment, hate speech, off-topic, impersonation, or other) and add an optional note. Flagged reports are reviewed by a moderator within 24 hours.

To block a walker entirely, open any of their reports and tap **Block this user** at the bottom. You'll be asked to type the word "block" to confirm. Their reports will no longer appear on your map, and you can unblock from **My Activity → Blocked users** at any time.

### How does moderation work?

- **Before anyone else sees a report,** an automated check reviews its text. If the report fails the check, or the check can't run, it stays hidden until a moderator looks at it. Borderline reports stay visible but go to a moderator, and their text is left out of alerts until they've been reviewed.
- **Flags are reviewed within 24 hours.** Anyone can flag a report with "Report this content".
- **Auto-hide:** if a flag is still open after 24 hours, the report is hidden automatically until a moderator decides.
- **Appeals:** if your report was removed and you think that was a mistake, email [privacy@paw-watch.app](mailto:privacy@paw-watch.app). We respond within five business days.

### How do I invite a walker to walk my dog?

Open Account, tap **Invites**, and tap **Invite a new PawWalker**. Pick the pets to share and you'll get a 6-character code (also shown as a QR code) to send them. After they sign in, they open your link or enter the code under Invites → **Have an invite code?** Their walker mode turns on automatically when they accept, and you can revoke it any time.
